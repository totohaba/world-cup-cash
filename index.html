How to Play

How to Win

Win the most fake money by correctly picking World Cup match winners.

Starting Balance

Every player starts with $200 in imaginary money.

$1 House Bet

Every submitted pick includes a free $1 house bet. You never lose that free dollar.

Adding Cash

You may add available fake cash to increase the amount risked on a pick.

Phase Locking

Each phase locks 1 hour before the first match begins.

Group-Stage Draws

If a group-stage match ends in a draw, picks on either team are marked as Draw.

Knockout Rounds

Pick the team that advances. Extra time and penalties count.

Odds Explanation

Higher odds mean a riskier pick with a bigger payout.

Tie-Breakers

Highest Current Balance wins, then most correct picks, then shared champion.
